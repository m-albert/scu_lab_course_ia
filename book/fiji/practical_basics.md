# P1.1: Fiji basics

## Preparation

1. Download this image to your computer.
1. Open the file in Fiji.

## Questions

Use Fiji to answer the following questions about the image:

1. What is the [pixel size](. "The physical size of a pixel in the image") of the image in microns?
1. What is the mean intensity of the image?
1. What's the intensity of the least and most bright pixel in the image?
1. What is the intensity of the pixel at position (100, 150)?
1. What's a good brightness/contrast setting for this image?
1. What is the width and height of the image in physical units?
